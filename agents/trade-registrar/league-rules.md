# Ultimate Guillotine Gulag League — the rules that bear on trades

Curated from `docs/rules/ultimate-guillotine-gulag-league-rules.docx`. Only the rules an agent
needs in order to read or price a trade are here; the full document is the authority. No member
is named.

## Shape of the league

- 18-team battle royale. The two lowest scorers each week drop into the gulag; after the gulag
  closes, the lowest of them is cut. $50 buy-in, winner takes all.
- Eliminated teams are out of the league, but the players they held are back in circulation, so a
  trade naming one of them is still a real trade.
- Week 12 cuts two (the gulag loser plus the lowest score overall); from week 13 on, the lowest
  score is cut each week until week 17's final head-to-head.

## The two budgets

- The auction draft budget is $200 a team.
- **Every $1 left unspent in the draft budget becomes $5 FAAB during the season.** Draft dollars
  and FAAB are the same money at 5:1 — $20 unspent is $100 FAAB — so a price quoted in draft
  dollars, draft FAAB or auction dollars is a FAAB price written the other way round.
- Every team is awarded a $10 FAAB survival bonus each week it is still alive.
- The commissioner may award up to about $10 FAAB a week for good memes, at his discretion.

## Waivers

- First round closes Thursday 2AM CST; second round closes Saturday 11AM CST.
- After each window FAAB is first-come, first-served.
- A player who has played, started playing, or is locked cannot be moved in any way, whatever the
  app allows. Bye-week players are not locked.
- Waiver bugs are the commissioner's to fix, by silent auction or by sending the player to free
  agency.

## Trading

- A trade must be submitted **in writing** and approved by the commissioner to be final. This is
  why announcements are posted to the chat at all.
- Both sides' players must be unlocked.
- While the gulag is active, trades are locked until the gulag drops have happened.
- Gulag teams may trade with teams outside the gulag.
- Almost anything short of collusion is allowed, on the honor system. The commissioner may veto a
  trade that is against fair competition, usually after consulting the league.
- Explicitly allowed and common: renting or swapping a player for an agreed period, renting for
  money, options on player ownership, buying a player outright, brokering a trade for a cut,
  derivative bets on another team's performance, paying somebody not to make a trade, and holding
  a Monday-night player to trade a roster spot.
- Explicitly disallowed: trading a roster spot or a player for no gain in order to hurt or
  eliminate another team, renting at a large discount when better offers exist, anything done in
  real life for fantasy gain, and survival-odds bets where a gulag team sends its last dollars
  away for a return if it survives.
- **No trade deadline is stated in the rules.**

## Gulag and keepers

- The bottom two teams lose every player except two they nominate as keepers, declared by 8PM CST
  Tuesday. They rebuild through waivers and trades that week and play a separate head-to-head.
- A team may pay somebody else to take its place as champion in the gulag; that person takes the
  gulag penalty.
- The gulag winner returns with the team they rebuilt. A Gladiator Bowl win is rewarded with one
  keeper; there is no Gladiator Bowl in an 18-team season.
